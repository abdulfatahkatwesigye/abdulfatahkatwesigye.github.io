01 Who I Am

Building people-first organisations.

Strategic HR practice, purposeful leadership, and systems that make people data useful.

Born on 2nd February 2000 in Uganda, I am a People Strategist, HR Practitioner, Human Capital Analyst, and Training and Development Specialist currently serving as an HR Associate at JobConnect Limited in Namanve, one of Uganda's leading HR consulting firms. I am also pursuing a Postgraduate Diploma in Human Resource Management at the Uganda Management Institute (2026–2027).

My career pairs professional HR practice with strong Management Information Systems (MIS) development and HR digitisation. I build and refine systems for employee data, payroll structures, contract generation, ID management, and compliance tracking across multiple client sites.

I am a Practicing Member of HRMAU (PHRMAU) and Past President of Rotaract Club of Upper Kibuli.

03 Systems Builder

Management Information Systems

Most HR problems are not people problems. They are information problems. Attendance is tracked in notebooks. Performance data lives in someone's inbox. Payroll is computed manually every month.

I build Management Information Systems that fix this, creating structured digital environments where HR data is captured accurately, processed efficiently, and presented as clear reports that help managers decide faster.

From Excel-based tracking tools to full database solutions, every system I build reflects how HR teams actually work on the ground in Uganda.
