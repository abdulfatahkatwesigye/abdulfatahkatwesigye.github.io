Management Information Systems

Bridging the gap between HR practice and data systems that actually work for organisations.

Most HR problems are not people problems. They are information problems. Attendance is tracked in notebooks. Performance data lives in someone's inbox. Payroll is computed manually every month.

I build Management Information Systems that fix this, creating structured digital environments where HR data is captured accurately, processed efficiently, and presented as clear reports that help managers make better decisions faster.

From Excel-based tracking tools to full database solutions, my MIS work is grounded in practical HR knowledge, which means every system I build reflects how HR teams actually work on the ground in Uganda.

  HR Technology & Systems
Employee Data Management
Centralised systems to store, manage, and retrieve employee records, contracts, and HR documentation with accuracy and speed.
Attendance & Leave Tracking
Automated attendance logging and leave management systems that reduce manual effort and eliminate errors in HR administration.
Performance Dashboards
Custom reporting dashboards that translate raw HR data into visual insights for performance reviews and management decisions.
Payroll & Compensation Systems
Structured payroll computation tools that reduce errors, enforce policy, and generate audit-ready reports for finance teams.
